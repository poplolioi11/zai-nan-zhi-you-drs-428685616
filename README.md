# 災難之由（DRS 428685616） / Zai nan zhi you: Transcription Dataset

《災難之由》是一張華文木刻論說印件。館目著錄為中國刊行，年代估在 1875–1911 年間。確切刊年、刊所與出版者未見，作者不詳。固定影像 p001 為轉錄底本，可見原刻篇題與由右至左的直排正文。p002 為同篇文字的對照影像。文章開篇以「上帝一大父也」「父於子無不愛」「上帝於人亦無不愛」，用父子親愛界說上帝愛人，隨即列舉兵戈、瘟疫、旱乾水溢等災禍。中段批評人倫及社會失序，文本主張「天怒而災害臨」，又說「然而上帝甚愛人」「殊不忍遽以災害降世」，將災害置於天怒、上帝愛人與警誡的論說脈絡，並勸人悔過遷善。篇末延續自省自修的勸說，以「吾願有心者其思之其細思之」作結。

本資料集以哈佛燕京藏本 DRS 428685616 為底本，共取 2 幅固定影像，其中 1 幅用於轉錄正文，提供 JSON、TXT、TEI 和逐項覆核紀錄。此版為 AI 校讀稿，21 項比對中保留 12 項未解。人工全文二校待完成。

Zai nan zhi you (災難之由) is a Chinese-language woodblock-printed broadside. The catalog records it as printed in China, with an estimated date between 1875 and 1911. The exact date, place of printing, publisher, and author are unknown. Fixed image p001 is the transcription source. It shows the printed title 災難之由 and the vertical text read from right to left. Image p002 is a comparison scan of the same text. The essay opens by defining Shangdi's love for humankind through the analogy of a father's love for his children ("上帝一大父也", "父於子無不愛", "上帝於人亦無不愛"), then lists calamities such as war, epidemics, droughts, and floods. In its middle section it criticizes disorder in human relationships and society. The text asserts that "when Heaven is angered, calamities descend" (天怒而災害臨), yet also that Shangdi greatly loves humankind and cannot bear to send down calamities hastily. The essay thus interprets disasters within a framework of divine anger, love, and admonition, and urges people to repent and reform. It closes by continuing its call for self-examination and self-cultivation, ending with a plea that thoughtful readers reflect carefully (吾願有心者其思之其細思之).

This dataset uses 2 fixed images from Harvard-Yenching Library DRS 428685616. It transcribes the main text from 1 page and documents the comparison image. The AI-reviewed JSON, TXT and TEI transcription retains 12 unresolved comparison items. Full human collation is outstanding.

## 資料與校讀

- data/document.json 保留歷史文字、原圖位置、疑讀與缺口。
- data/text.txt 與 data/text.tei.xml 為全文與 TEI，附文字單位的對應檔。
- data/review.json 保留 21 項比對、12 項未解及採用紀錄。
- data/manifest.json 與 PACKAGE-SHA256SUMS.txt 記錄固定發行的檔案雜湊。

館目著錄木刻單張。固定 PDF 收錄同篇兩幅影像，p001 作轉錄底本，含原刻篇題及 29 個正文欄，未見原刊記。p002 作同篇對照，公開資料保留其影像次序與版面定位。

Claude 分別依 p001 完成初錄與隔離的第二稿，再依原圖覆核程式列出的 21 項比對。採入 3 項有影像依據的更正，保留未解位置與候選。程式驗收確認公開 JSON、TXT 和 TEI 的逐欄內容一致。TEI 通過 P5 4.12.0 Relax NG。人工全文二校、專家審定和準確率量測待完成。

Claude produced separate readings of p001 and reviewed 21 comparison observations against the source images. The edition incorporates 3 corrections supported by the images. Automated validation confirmed that the transcription content agrees column by column across JSON, TXT and TEI. The TEI passed P5 4.12.0 Relax NG validation. Full human collation, expert assessment and accuracy measurement are outstanding.
